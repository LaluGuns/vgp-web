same-snare|Same snare. Same compressor.
listen|Listen.
flat|One sounds flat.
punchy|One sounds punchy.
knob|And only ONE knob changed.
hand|Picture a tiny hand inside the compressor, resting on a fader.
watch|It watches the level.
pull|When a hit crosses the threshold... the hand pulls the fader down.
parts|But every hit has two parts:
crack|a sharp crack at the front,
body|and a longer body behind it.
attack|Attack is how fast the hand grabs.
fast|At one millisecond, it catches the crack — and squashes it.
slow|At thirty milliseconds, it's late on purpose.
slips|Most of the crack slips past, and the body gets turned down.
punch|That's punch.
release|Release is how fast the hand lets go.
hold|Let go too slowly, and it's still holding on when the next hit arrives.
squashed|So the next crack gets squashed too.
fresh|Let go in time, and every hit starts fresh.
rule-a|Attack shapes the hit.
rule-r|Release shapes the groove.
again|Now listen again.
