[warmly] Picture a tiny hand inside the compressor, resting on a fader. It watches the level. When a hit crosses the threshold... the hand pulls the fader down.

But every hit has two parts: a sharp crack at the front, and a longer body behind it.

Attack is how fast the hand grabs.

At one millisecond, it catches the crack — and squashes it.

[mischievously] At thirty milliseconds, it's late on purpose. Most of the crack slips past, and the body gets turned down. [excited] That's punch.

[warmly] Release is how fast the hand lets go.

Let go too slowly, and it's still holding on when the next hit arrives. So the next crack gets squashed too.

Let go in time, and every hit starts fresh.

[confident] Attack shapes the hit. Release shapes the groove.
