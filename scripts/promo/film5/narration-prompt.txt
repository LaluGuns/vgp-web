[excited] Number two. It has a gap: under a quarter second of silence before the kick. [curious] Why? Your ears, your limiter, your brain.

In number one, the riser is still roaring when the kick lands, so it covers the click. [warmly] In number two, the riser stops, its after-fog fades, and your ears meet the kick fresh.

[mischievously] Bonus: your limiter, a little hand on a fader. The riser makes it pull the whole mix down as the kick lands.

[confident] And your brain: the silence leaves one thing to predict, the next beat, so the kick lands as a payoff.

So cut everything an eighth note before the drop, reverb tails too. A sixteenth already helps, but the after-fog can last a fifth of a second, and at 128 BPM, only an eighth outlasts it.
