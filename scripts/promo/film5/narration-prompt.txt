[excited] Number two. Same drop, but with a hole in it: less than a quarter second of silence right before the kick.

[curious] A loud riser dulls your hearing for up to a fifth of a second after it stops. In number one, the kick's click lands right inside that fog.

[warmly] After silence, your ears give the next sound their full response.

[mischievously] Your limiter is a little hand on a fader, and the riser has it pulling everything down right as the kick arrives.

[confident] Silence also leaves your brain one thing to predict: the next beat. Land the kick right there, and the arrival is the payoff.
