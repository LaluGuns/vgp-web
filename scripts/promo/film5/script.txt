guess|One or two? Lock in your pick. The answer is at the end.
hook|Number two has a gap: under a quarter second of silence before the kick. It works on three things: your ears, your limiter, your brain.
fog|In number one, the riser is still roaring when the kick lands, so it covers the click.
fresh|In number two, the riser stops, its after-fog fades, and your ears meet the kick fresh.
hand|Then your limiter, a little hand on a fader. The riser makes it pull the whole mix down as the kick lands.
brain|And your brain: the silence leaves one thing to predict, the next beat, so the kick lands as a payoff.
how|So cut everything about a quarter second before the drop, reverb tails too. The after-fog can last two hundred milliseconds, and at 128 BPM, a thirty-second and a sixteenth fall short; an eighth outlasts it.
again|Now listen again. Number two hits harder. Hear the click?
