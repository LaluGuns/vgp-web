fog|In number one, the riser is still roaring when the kick lands, so it covers the click.
fresh|In number two, the riser stops, its after-fog fades, and your ears meet the kick fresh.
hand|Bonus: your limiter, a little hand on a fader. The riser makes it pull the whole mix down as the kick lands.
brain|And your brain: the silence leaves one thing to predict, the next beat, so the kick lands as a payoff.
how|So cut everything about a quarter second before the drop, reverb tails too. The after-fog can last two hundred milliseconds, and at 128 BPM, an eighth note is the shortest gap that outlasts it.
